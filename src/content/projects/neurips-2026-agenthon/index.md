The **NeurIPS 2026 Agenthon** (organized by the Society of Quantitative Analysts, CEWIT, and Bloomberg CTO Office) is a global competition focused on autonomous, verifiable AI agents for quantitative finance.

I am an active contender across all four challenge tracks:
* **Coding:** Automated quantitative code generation, verification, and execution.
* **Forecasting:** Probabilistic time-series forecasting with calibrated confidence bounds.
* **Simulation:** High-throughput limit-order book market simulation using the ABIDES runtime.
* **Explainability & Verification:** Verifiable, anytime-valid decision metrics and automated data leakage controls.

The system is architected as Docker-sandboxed autonomous agents equipped with strict temporal leakage guards, reproducible execution harnesses, and formal evaluation metrics suitable for mission-critical financial applications.
